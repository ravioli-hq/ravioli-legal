Delete Your Account & Data

Ravioli · Ravi Inc.

You can request deletion of your Ravioli account and all data associated with it at any time.

How to request deletion

Email support@ravi.id from, or referencing, the phone number you registered with, and ask us to delete your account. We use the phone number to locate and verify your account before deleting it.

What is deleted

We delete your account and the personal data tied to it, including your phone number, the profile details you provided, and your conversation history and any associated stored memory.

Timing

Requests are processed within 30 days. Data required to be retained for legal or security purposes may be kept for the period the law requires, then deleted.

See also our Privacy Policy.
