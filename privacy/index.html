Privacy Policy

Ravioli · Effective October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ravi Inc. ("Ravi", "we", "us") handles information in connection with the Ravioli mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

We collect information needed to provide and operate the App:

  • Account information. A phone number, which we use to create and secure your account and to sign you in.
  • Profile information. Details you choose to provide when you set up or use the App, so we can personalize your experience.
  • Content you provide. The messages and other content you submit while using the App, so the App can respond and function as intended.
  • Voice and audio. When you start a voice call or record a voice note in the App, we access your microphone and transmit your audio in real time so the conversation can happen. That audio is processed to convert speech to text and to generate the companion's spoken reply, which also produces a transcript of the call. We access your microphone only during a call or recording that you start.
  • Contacts. The current single-companion app does not request contacts access or upload your address book. Earlier versions offered optional friend matching. If you used that feature, the App used your device address book to find which of your contacts already use Ravioli, so you can start a chat with them. Ravioli is designed to work the same wherever you sign in — the same chats and the same people, on your phone or on the web — so this information is stored with your account rather than on a single device, and it also lets us tell you when someone you know joins and label your notifications. Contacts access is optional, and the rest of the App works without it. You can ask us to delete this information at any time — see Your Choices and Rights below.
  • Notification tokens. If you enable notifications, we register your device's push token along with the platform and app version so we can deliver messages and reminders to that device.
  • Usage and device information. Basic technical and diagnostic information about how the App is used and the device it runs on — including app version, platform, and approximate time zone — which helps us keep the App reliable and improve it.

Permissions We Request

The App asks for a device permission only when a feature needs it, and you may decline or later revoke any of them in your device settings:

  • Microphone — for a voice call or voice note that you start.
  • Contacts — used by the optional friend-matching feature in earlier versions. The current app does not request this permission.
  • Notifications — to deliver new messages and reminders.

Declining a permission disables the feature that needs it; it does not stop you from using the rest of the App.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the App; to provide and personalize its features; to communicate with you; and to comply with legal obligations.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to run the App:

  • With service providers who process information on our behalf and under our instructions to deliver the App's functionality — including cloud hosting, AI service providers, real-time voice transport, push-notification delivery, phone-number verification, and product analytics. These providers may process your information only to provide services to us, under contract and on our instructions. They may not use it for their own purposes. See AI Features below.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users, the public, or Ravi.
  • In connection with a business transaction such as a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, subject to this Policy.

AI Features and Service Providers

Jino is an AI companion. To create replies and maintain conversation memory, Ravioli processes the messages, voice notes, call audio, transcripts, profile information, and conversation memories you provide. Personal details in this content can be included in the data sent to the following services. The services used depend on the feature and our provider settings. Listing a provider does not mean that every conversation is sent to that provider:

  • Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic: these are possible AI providers. The selected provider receives messages, call transcripts, relevant conversation memories, and profile details such as your name, date of birth, and gender to generate replies and maintain memory.
  • LiveKit: carries real-time call audio and connects the call to AI and speech services.
  • Deepgram: receives call audio through LiveKit to convert speech to text.
  • ElevenLabs: receives voice-note audio to convert it to text, and receives reply text to generate speech.
  • LangChain: receives conversation content and technical traces for service diagnostics, error analysis, and quality checks.

These services process data to provide the functions described above. We require service providers to protect shared data to the same standard described in this policy and to use it only to provide services to us. We do not sell conversation content or share it for advertising. We do not send your address book to these AI services.

The current app shows these recipients, data types, and purposes before asking you to select Allow and continue. Selecting Not now signs you out without starting a conversation. The app requires this choice for both new and returning accounts before it enables chat and calls. The choice is stored for your account on your device and is cleared when you sign out. A change to the disclosed recipients or uses requires a new choice.

You can stop sending new conversation data by ending a call, stopping a recording, and no longer sending messages. You can sign out in Settings or use Delete account in Settings to request deletion of your account and associated data. The AI features cannot operate without the processing specified above. We update this policy when the services or uses change.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as it is needed to provide the App. When you delete your account, everything associated with it is deleted — we retain nothing about you afterwards. See Your Choices and Rights below for how to request deletion.

Your Choices and Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, including your account and associated data, by contacting us at support@ravi.id or by using our account and data deletion request page. We will respond consistent with applicable law.

You can also revoke the microphone, contacts, or notification permissions at any time in your device settings. Revoking contacts access stops the App from sending further contact information; to have contact information we already hold deleted, use the deletion request above.

Security

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

International Users

We may process and store information in countries other than where you live. Where we do, we take steps consistent with applicable law.

Changes to This Policy
